# Spec: ShopExpansion (Epic34)

## Overview / Goal

More long-term goals in the wardrobe: ten more accessories with rising prices and five super accessories that need very many points.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Justification |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| New accessories | 10 items, prices rising from 6000 to 15000 points, spread over the slots head, eyes, and neck | Continues the existing price curve (max. 5000) |
| Super accessories | 5 items for 25000, 30000, 35000, 40000, and 50000 points (from 5x the previous maximum) | A long-term goal that keeps the child reading |
| Look | Super accessories are marked in the wardrobe (golden frame, "Super" label) and sparkle on the dino | They should feel special |
| Graphics | New hand-made SVGs under `assets/svg/accessories/` in the existing style | Same as the existing accessories |

## Acceptance Criteria

- 15 new buyable accessories with SVGs; point rewards stay sorted by price.
- Super accessories are visibly marked in the wardrobe and sparkle when worn.
- Existing tests pass.
